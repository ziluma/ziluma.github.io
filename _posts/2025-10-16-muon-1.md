Muon works on matrix parameters $W\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times d}.$
WLOG assume $d<n$.

$$
\begin{align*}
M_t&= \beta M_{t-1} + \nabla \ell_t(M_{t-1}),\\
W_t &= W_{t-1} - \eta(\pi(M_{t})+\lambda W_{t-1}).
\end{align*}
$$

Here $\pi(M)$ denotes the projection of $M$ onto $V_{n,d}$ with respect to the usual Frobenius norm, i.e.,

$$
|M-\pi(M)| = \min_{V\in V_{n,d}} |M-V|_{\rm F}
$$

where F denotes the usual Frobenius norm and

$$
V_{n,d} :=\Big\lbrace V\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times d}:V^\top V = I\Big\rbrace.
$$
